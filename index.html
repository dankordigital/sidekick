Questions

Short answers about price, setup and your data.

Is Sidekick free?expand_more

Yes, with every feature, on every device. There's no account and no subscription.

Do I need an account or the internet?expand_more

No. Your devices talk to each other directly, on your own Wi-Fi or a direct Wi-Fi link between the two. Nothing goes through our servers, because there aren't any.

How do I connect my phone and computer?expand_more

Open Sidekick on both. Tap Pair next to the other device and type the 6-digit code it shows, or scan its QR code with your phone. They remember each other from then on.

What if we're not on the same Wi-Fi?expand_more

Sidekick links the two devices directly: one opens a small Wi-Fi network and the other joins it. iPhones and Macs use Apple's own direct link, like AirDrop.

Is it safe?expand_more

Everything is encrypted, and only devices you paired with the code can connect. A paired device can only do what you allow in Settings.

Can my computer control my iPhone?expand_more

No: Apple doesn't let any app do that. Your iPhone can control your computer, and everything else works on iPhone too.

Where do the files go?expand_more

Into a Sidekick folder you can change in Settings. On phones, photos and videos go straight to Photos or the gallery.

When can I get it?expand_more

Soon. Join the wishlist and we'll email you once, the day it's out.
