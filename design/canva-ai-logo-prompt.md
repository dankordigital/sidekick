Design 10 different logo concepts for an app called **Sidekick**.

**What Sidekick is**
Sidekick makes your phone and your computer work as one. You can send files, photos, links and notes both ways, use your phone as your computer's touchpad and keyboard, ring a lost phone, and copy sign-in codes from your phone on your computer. It works on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone. The two devices talk to each other directly, with no account and no cloud, and everything is encrypted. It's free, calm and private: a helpful companion that is always at your side, never in your way.

**Who it's for**
Everyday people with a phone and a laptop: students, families, people who work from home. It's not aimed at tech experts. The brand should feel friendly, trustworthy and simple, like a good tool rather than a gadget.

**The look of the app**
Dark and minimal: a near-black background (#121212), dark grey panels (#1c1c1c), white rounded pill buttons, and colour only where it means something. The app's accent colours are blue #5b9dff, green #4fcf86, amber #ffc56b and orange #ffb84d. The font is Inter.

**What the logo must do**
- Be a simple **abstract symbol** (a mark), plus a version with the word "Sidekick" next to it in a clean bold sans-serif like Inter.
- Express **two things that belong together**: a pair, a partner at your side, two parts making one whole, a connection or a handover.
- Be **symmetrical or balanced**, with **rounded, soft edges**. Use no more than 2 colours.
- Work as an **app icon** (a rounded square) and still be clear at **16 pixels**, as a browser tab or menu-bar icon.
- Work in white on dark and in dark on white.

**Please avoid**
- Letters or monograms ("S", "SK").
- Drawings of phones, laptops, screens, cables, Wi-Fi waves, Bluetooth symbols, arrows or chat bubbles.
- Dots or circles arranged like planets, blobs, or anything that looks like a pause button (two plain bars).
- A circle split in two halves (too close to SumUp) and two overlapping squares (too close to copyui).
- Gradients, glows, 3D effects, mascots or characters.

**10 directions, one logo each**
1. **Pair**: two rounded shapes standing side by side, one slightly taller, like two friends.
2. **Embrace**: two curved shapes wrapping around a shared centre.
3. **Hinge**: two rounded panels joined at one edge, like a book opening.
4. **Lock**: two shapes that interlock like puzzle halves, without looking like a puzzle piece.
5. **Bloom**: one shape made of two mirrored petals.
6. **Handover**: two rounded forms passing a small shape from one to the other.
7. **Bridge**: two pillars joined by one soft arc.
8. **Echo**: one shape and its slightly smaller twin following it.
9. **Knot**: one continuous rounded line that forms two connected loops.
10. **Side by side**: two capsules leaning together at the top.

For each concept, show:
- the mark alone on #121212,
- the mark as an app icon,
- the mark with "Sidekick" beside it.

Give each concept its name, and keep them clearly different from each other.
