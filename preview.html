Lemon drizzle cake

225 g butter, 225 g caster sugar, 4 eggs, 275 g self-raising flour, the zest of 1 lemon.

Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Beat the butter and sugar until pale and creamy.

2. Add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and the zest.

3. Pour into a lined loaf tin and bake for 45 to 50 minutes.

The drizzle

Mix the juice of 1½ lemons with 85 g caster sugar and spoon it over the warm cake.

Leave it in the tin until cool. Enjoy!
